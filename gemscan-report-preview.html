What it is

A heterogeneous dark grey-green matrix with olivine xenocrysts and lithic mantle inclusions, typical of a volcanic breccia called kimberlite.

Matoore madow-cagaaran oo aan isku ekayn, leh xenocrysts olivine ah iyo qaybo mantle ah — sida caadiga ah kimberlite.

Commonly hosts

The primary world source for natural diamond, rich in mantle indicator minerals: red pyrope garnet, bright green chrome diopside, chromite and picroilmenite.

Isha koowaad ee dheemanka dabiiciga ah, oo hodan ku ah macdano tilmaame: pyrope garnet cas, chrome diopside cagaar ah, chromite iyo ilmenite.

Geological environment

Kimberlite pipes erupt through ancient continental cratons; while mapped as Cenozoic sediment here, intrusions often pierce younger cover.
