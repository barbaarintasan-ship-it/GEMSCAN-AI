Delete Your GemScan Account

Last updated: 15 July 2026

This page explains how to delete your GemScan account and the data associated with it. It applies to the GemScan mobile app published by GemScan (barbaarintasan.com).

Option 1 — Delete your account inside the app (instant)

  1. Open the GemScan app.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (top right of the home screen).
  3. Open Account.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email barbaarintasan@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my GemScan account”. We will verify your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and login (email address);
  • Your profile details (name, phone number, country, city);
  • All of your scans and the photos you uploaded;
  • The location data attached to your scans;
  • Your subscription record.

What data is kept, and for how long

We do not keep your personal data after deletion. Residual copies in encrypted backups are automatically purged within 30 days. We may retain a minimal, non-identifying record of a payment/transaction only where required by law (for example, tax or accounting obligations), for the period required by that law.

Contact
