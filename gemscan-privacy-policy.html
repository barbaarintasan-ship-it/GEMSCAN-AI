Privacy Policy for GemScan

Last updated: 15 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GemScan (“GemScan”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the GemScan mobile application (the “App”) and the related website at barbaarintasan.com (together, the “Service”). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account information

When you create an account we collect the details you provide, which may include your email address, full name, phone number, country, and city. This is used to create and secure your account and to identify the correct account for subscription features.

b. Photos you submit

When you scan or upload an item, the App captures or receives the photograph(s) you provide. These images are used to perform the identification, market-value estimate, and related features you requested.

c. Location information

If you grant location permission, the App records the GPS location where a scan is taken, so that you can see your finds on a personal map inside the App. Location is optional — you can decline the permission or turn it off at any time in your device settings, and the App will continue to work without it.

d. Usage and device information

We may collect limited technical and diagnostic information (such as app version, device type, general performance and error data) to keep the Service reliable and to fix problems.

We do not collect payment card numbers in the App. Any subscription purchase is completed on our website through a secure third-party payment provider; we never see or store your full card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core identification and valuation features you request;
  • To create, secure, and manage your account and subscription;
  • To display your scan history and your finds on your personal map;
  • To let you contact an expert for a professional review, when you choose to;
  • To operate, maintain, improve, and troubleshoot the Service;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with trusted service providers who process it on our behalf and only as needed to run the Service:

  • Cloud hosting & database provider — stores your account, scans, and images securely on our behalf.
  • Specialist analysis providers — your submitted photograph is processed by secure third-party analysis services to generate the identification and valuation results. Images are processed to deliver your result and are not used to identify you personally.
  • Payment provider — when you purchase a subscription on our website, a secure payment processor handles the transaction.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or to prevent fraud or abuse.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service.

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the App: go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your scans, your images, and related data from our systems. You may also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls that restrict each account’s data to that account. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country. By using the Service, you consent to such processing in accordance with this Policy.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise the deletion right in-app (Section 4) or by contacting us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
